Process-driven

Repeatable, systematized triggers
  • Out-of-office reply: send a coffee, and schedule a week’s worth for when they return, with no ask attached.
  • Pre-demo: send a coffee the morning of, alongside the calendar invite.

Relationship-driven

Personal signals you notice and react to
  • A dog going crazy on a call: send dog treats.
  • A college football jersey in someone’s background: send a football-themed “Latte of Luck” at the start of the season.
